## The line

> My neighbour's cat, who has ignored me for a whole year, chose today at 6 a.m. to climb onto my lap and fall asleep mid-purr.

## The entry

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, no network, no randomness) writes that
one line to `/output/entry.txt` — deterministically, the same bytes on
every run. The line is 125 characters, inside the 200-character gate.
A judge model compares lines two at a time, weighing warmth first, then
surprise, then one concrete detail, then fresh wording. The warmth here
is a year of indifference ending in sudden trust; the surprise is the
timing — today, at 6 a.m. — and the cat falling asleep mid-purr; the
concrete detail is the hour. The top three share $5.
